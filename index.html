How it works

From brief to delivery in four steps

  1. Brief

    A short call or email about what you need, who it is for, where it will be used and when it is due. You get a fixed quote.

  2. Plan and script

    We write the script or interview questions, plan the shot list and sort locations, talent and schedules with your team.

  3. Shoot

    A small, organised crew on the day. We work around your staff and your site rules, not the other way round.

  4. Edit and deliver

    One round of feedback is built in. Final files are delivered in the formats you need, from 16:9 to vertical cut-downs.
